# Tikko privacy policy

_Last updated: November 2026_

Tikko is a time-tracking app. We built it to keep your data on your phone.

## What Tikko stores
- Your check-ins (which activity you did each hour, and any short detail you type), your settings and your answers to the intro questions.
- All of this is stored **only on your device**. It is not sent to us or to anyone else, and deleting the app deletes it. You can also erase it any time in **Settings → Erase all data**.

## Notifications
Tikko schedules hourly check-in reminders on your device. They are created locally; no notification content leaves your phone.

## Subscriptions
Purchases are handled by Apple. Tikko uses RevenueCat to check whether your subscription is active. RevenueCat receives an anonymous app user ID and your purchase receipts from Apple, but no check-in data. See RevenueCat's privacy policy at https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy.

## What we don't do
- No accounts, no tracking across apps, no ads, and no selling of data.
