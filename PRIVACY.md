# Tikko privacy policy

_Last updated: November 2026_

Tikko is a time-tracking app. Your day stays on your phone. We only collect a small amount of anonymous usage data to improve the app.

## Stored only on your iPhone
Your check-ins (which activity you did each hour), the details you type, your settings and your recaps are stored **only on your iPhone**. The details you type never leave your phone. Deleting the app deletes this data. You can also erase it any time in **Settings → Erase all data**.

## Anonymous usage analytics (PostHog)
We use PostHog to understand how Tikko is used. It receives, tied to a random ID (not your name or email):
- **How you use the app:** screens opened, intro steps reached, taps on Tikko, notifications opened.
- **Check-ins:** the type of activity you chose (for example "Deep work"), how many hours, and whether you added a detail (never the detail itself).
- **Your intro answers:** your goal, what describes you, how many hours you think you lose, what you'd do with time back, your time sinks and where you heard about Tikko. Your age is not sent.
- **How each day felt:** the 1–5 rating in the evening recap.
- **Subscription events:** viewing the paywall, the plan you chose, its price, and whether a trial started.
- **Basic device information:** device model, iOS version, app version, language and time zone.

PostHog is set to discard IP addresses. See https://posthog.com/privacy.

## Subscriptions (Apple and RevenueCat)
Payments are handled by Apple; we never see your card details. Tikko uses RevenueCat to check whether your subscription is active. RevenueCat receives a random app user ID, your purchase history from Apple and, if you answered, where you heard about Tikko, but none of your check-ins. See https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy.

## Notifications
Check-ins, the evening recap and the trial reminder are scheduled on your device. Their content never leaves your phone.

## What we don't do
No accounts, no ads, no tracking across other companies' apps or websites, and no selling of data.
